Camera consent — please read

The photos in this gallery are protected by copyright. To protect them, this page uses your device's camera only as a local sensor: it checks that a person is facing the screen and that no phone or other camera is pointed at it.

No image, video or sound from your camera is recorded, stored, or sent to us or to anyone else. It is processed in real time, only inside this browser on this device, and is discarded immediately. We cannot see you. No face recognition or identification is performed — the page only checks whether a face and a phone are present.

The camera is used only while this page is open. Closing the tab turns it off, and you can withdraw permission at any time in your browser's settings.

By selecting Allow camera you consent to this processing for this purpose. If you do not allow it, the photos stay hidden and nothing else changes.

Photographing, recording, screenshotting or otherwise reproducing these photos without a licence from the photographer is not permitted.

Without the camera the photos stay hidden.
